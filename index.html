Inset surface web

Each face is inset in its own plane. The centre opening is removed and the remaining border is welded into a single exported mesh. Width is reduced locally if a face would collapse.

Triangulated tubes

For each edge shared by exactly two faces, the original edge and two face-aligned inset lines form a triangular cross-section. Non-manifold edges are skipped and reported.
